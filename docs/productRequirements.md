# Product Requirements: Groww Feedback Intelligence Dashboard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** [problemStatement.md](problemStatement.md), [architecture.md](architecture.md), [implementationPlan.md](implementationPlan.md)

## 1. Overview
The Groww Feedback Intelligence Dashboard is a web application designed to sit on top of the Groww Weekly User Feedback Intelligence AI Agent. Its primary purpose is to present the rich, AI-analyzed data to leadership teams in a highly actionable format, while also providing administrative controls to manage the underlying AI agent's configuration and operations.

## 2. Leadership Presentation Views
The dashboard must present the data in a way that allows leadership to quickly understand app health, identify critical issues, and track trends without getting bogged down in raw data.

### 2.1 The "Week at a Glance" Executive Summary (Hero Section)
*   **Overall App Health Score:** A composite score based on average star rating and positive/negative sentiment ratio.
*   **Key Volume Metrics:** Total reviews analyzed this week, week-over-week (WoW) volume change.
*   **Top Positive & Negative Signals:** The single most praised aspect and the single most critical pain point of the week.
*   **AI-Generated Executive Summary:** A brief 5-bullet summary generated by the AI highlighting critical shifts.

### 2.2 Sentiment & Rating Trends (Time-Series)
*   **Sentiment Distribution Breakdown:** Visual representation (stacked bar or donut chart) showing the percentage of Positive, Neutral, Negative, and Mixed reviews for the current period.
*   **WoW Sentiment Shift:** A line graph tracking the Net Sentiment Score (Positive % - Negative %) over the last 4–12 weeks.
*   **Rating vs. Sentiment Correlation:** Analysis showing if star ratings accurately reflect textual sentiment.

### 2.3 Theme Discovery & Priority Matrix
*   **The Action Matrix (Severity vs. Frequency):** A scatter plot mapping themes based on frequency (user volume) and severity (AI-scored language). The top-right quadrant (High Frequency, High Severity) highlights immediate action items.
*   **Top User Themes List:** A ranked list of major themes (e.g., "Login/OTP Issues") displaying:
    *   Theme Name
    *   Review Count & % of total
    *   Priority Signal (Critical, High, Medium, Low)
    *   WoW Trend (Growing, Declining, Persistent)

### 2.4 Emerging Signals & Early Warnings
*   **Rapidly Growing Pain Points:** Themes with a sudden spike in negative volume compared to historical data.
*   **New Themes Discovered:** Issues detected for the very first time in the current week.
*   **Declining Issues:** A section showing previously high-priority issues that are successfully fading away.

### 2.5 Product Area Deep Dives
*   **Category Breakdown:** Filtering capabilities to view data by specific product areas (e.g., Stocks, Mutual Funds, IPOs, Onboarding).
*   **Feature Requests Board:** A dedicated view aggregating frequent user requests, ranked by volume and potential impact.

### 2.6 The "Voice of the Customer" (Qualitative Evidence)
*   **Representative Excerpts:** A curated carousel of exact, unedited user quotes for every theme or metric, proving that insights are grounded in real user pain.

### 2.7 Recommended Next Actions
*   **AI-Suggested Roadmap:** Actionable recommendations generated by the agent (e.g., "Investigate UX flow for KYC onboarding").
*   **Ownership Assignment:** Inferred team ownership (Engineering, Support, Product) for specific action items.

## 3. Administrative Controls
To ensure the dashboard is maintainable and adaptable, a dedicated control panel is required for admins (Product Managers, AI Engineers, Ops teams) to manage the AI Agent's behavior.

### 3.1 Execution & Pipeline Controls
*   **Manual Trigger / "Run Now":** Ability to force a pipeline execution outside of the scheduled cron job.
*   **Pipeline Status Dashboard:** A live view of the current/last run status across all stages (Collection → Normalization → Classification → Theme Discovery → Generation → Delivery).
*   **Run History & Logs:** Access to execution reports and structured JSON logs for debugging past runs.

### 3.2 Collection & Data Source Management
*   **App Target Configuration:** Ability to update the target application identifier or URL.
*   **Data Volume Controls:** UI to adjust maximum reviews processed and minimum review thresholds for analysis.
*   **Reporting Period Adjustment:** Toggle reporting periods (Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly).

### 3.3 AI & Taxonomy Tuning
*   **Custom Category Manager:** Interface to add, edit, or remove categories from the taxonomy.
*   **Priority Weighting Sliders:** Controls to adjust the weights used in priority scoring (e.g., increasing the weight of negative sentiment).
*   **Model Selection & Prompts:** Dropdowns to switch underlying LLM models and adjust parameters like temperature.

### 3.4 Delivery & Destination Settings
*   **Recipient Management:** Manage the list of email recipients for the generated draft.
*   **Output Toggles:** Switches to independently enable/disable delivery channels (Docs vs. Gmail).
*   **Target Document Configuration:** Update the target Google Doc ID for report appending.

### 3.5 AI "Correction" & Ground Truth Management
*   **Theme Override / Merge:** Ability to manually merge duplicate themes discovered by the AI to ensure accurate historical tracking.
*   **Feedback Loop:** Mechanism to flag incorrect AI classifications (e.g., false positives on sentiment) to build a dataset for future model fine-tuning.
